Justin Edit — Visual and Audio Style Guide

Footage and transitions

The presenter’s picture and dialogue from Camera-Recording.mp4 lead the video. Waterfall footage from IMG_0133.MOV and ocean footage from IMG_0261.MOV provide video-only cutaways over the continuing presenter dialogue; omit both clips’ source audio. The waterfall cutaway lasts approximately five seconds and opens with a brief dip to white. The ocean footage has smooth, stabilized motion.

Presenter framing

A selected talking-head segment uses a modest punch-in for a tighter view of the presenter. Keep the remaining presenter segments at their standard framing.

Opening lower-third

The opening name graphic reads JUSTIN BROWN in white uppercase Oswald Bold. Place it horizontally centered in the lower third of the presenter image. A solid, fully opaque bright blue rectangle closely surrounds the lettering, with a small visible margin around the letters. Keep the name visible beyond the first five seconds.

Camera color

Use a warm, bright camera treatment with enhanced contrast and vibrant saturation. Keep this appearance consistent across the edited presenter shots while retaining each segment’s individual framing.

Audio

Keep the presenter’s speech clear and prominent. ES_San Pedro - Sugoi.mp3 provides quiet background music from the opening picture to the end of the video. Maintain dialogue and music as separate audio elements, with the presenter clips identified as Dialogue and the background track as Music.
