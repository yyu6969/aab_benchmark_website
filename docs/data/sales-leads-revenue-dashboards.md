You are a sales operations specialist setting up a new sales workspace and revenue tracking for Call Solutions LLC. Register and activate your workspace account through email verification using the following profile details:  
 - Name: Emily Call  
 - Role: Developer  
 - Company: Call Solutions LLC  
 - Email: [sfemilycall@gmail.com](mailto:sfemilycall@gmail.com)  
 - Username: [EEC@callcompany.com](mailto:EEC@callcompany.com)  
 - Location: Utah, United States, 98002

Within the sales workspace, client ABC's deal records should reflect the conversion of Jane Doe's Web lead in the Education industry, linking the ABC account and Jane Doe's contact record to the $100,000 "ABC- Initial Contract" opportunity at the Closed Won stage. Record John Doe as an additional contact under the ABC account.

For ongoing pipeline management, set up a private "My Open Leads" view showing only your active, unconverted leads and excluding any closed or converted records.

For team-wide revenue visibility, prepare a public "Closed Won Opportunities" report summarizing total closed-won opportunity revenue across all close dates, grouped by lead source. Keep the detail columns focused by including the last modified date while omitting owner role, age, and next step, and include a donut chart breaking down closed-won revenue by lead source.

Build a private companion dashboard named "Closed Won Dashboard" sourced from that report, displaying both an overall closed-won revenue metric and a donut chart breakdown by lead source with current figures loaded across all components (including the closed ABC contract). Set up a dashboard email subscription to deliver updates every Monday at 7:00 AM.
