## **10. Draft a Floor Plan and Architectural Details**

### **Task instruction**

You are an architectural drafter preparing a ground-floor plan and detail documentation package. Continue `/home/ga/Documents/CAD/floor plan exercise.dwg` in meters, following `/home/ga/Documents/CAD/floor plan exercise reference.pdf` as the design reference for the floor plan geometry, circulation, fixtures, room identities, and architectural details.

Organize the drawing cleanly across dedicated layers for building elements, fixtures, room labels, dimensions, and non-plotting viewports. Build reusable components with interactive controls where elements repeat across varied layouts: flippable single-door and private-restroom assemblies for opposing orientations, a symmetrical double-door component, and an adjustable-width window component that fits the varying opening widths. Label all rooms and residential units, and dimension the exterior plan and the sheeted details in meters using annotative dimensions scaled for their respective views.

Prepare two presentation sheets with rectangular sheet borders, title blocks, and hidden (non-plotting) viewport frames:  
- `Layout1`: Present the ground-floor plan at `1:200`. Complete the title block with `Project`, `Location`, `Drawing name`, `Drawn`, `Scale`, and `Date` fields, recording Project `Example 1`, Drawing name `Ground floor`, and Scale `1:200`.  
- `details`: Present two dimensioned detail views side by side—the single-door detail at `1:16` and the window/profile detail at `1:10`.

Save the completed editable drawing in place at `/home/ga/Documents/CAD/floor plan exercise.dwg` and publish the `Layout1` ground-floor sheet as a PDF at `/home/ga/Documents/CAD/example 2.pdf`.
