Task instruction

You are a project manager setting up internal team operations and preparing a holiday website launch for Apple.

The MinorCo workspace needs a new Marketing team that includes Warwick Palm alongside you. In the Asana Demo workspace, set up a workspace-accessible "Apple - Website Design" project where Warwick Palm and Lindsay Amoroso have editing access. Its project overview should capture the scope and business goal: designing a new website for Apple that must be live before Christmas to increase conversion rates and help sell more iPhones this holiday season.

Across client onboarding, design and planning, production, and go-live and final delivery, the project plan should make clear who owns each task across the team, when work is scheduled, how multi-part deliverables break down into subtasks, which approval and readiness milestones matter, and which tasks or testing milestones depend on earlier handoffs. Leave the production milestone for completed page designs open and ask Warwick Palm in its discussion to review the designs you have completed. Publish an on-track status update letting the team know the project is progressing well to finish before Christmas and summarizing the remaining incomplete tasks by assignee.

Keep your personal task list in Asana Demo organized by section with "Recently assigned" cleared, moving "Planning session with client" along with your newly assigned project commitments into "Upcoming". In MinorCo, follow up on two team updates: add a thumbs-up reaction to Warwick Palm's latest comment on "Troubleshoot: Pipedrive Document sync settings" and archive that notification once addressed, and express appreciation for Kayla Campos's completion of "Session #3 Team Follow Up" in its task discussion.
