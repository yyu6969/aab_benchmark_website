Task instruction

You are a video editor preparing an editable YouTube video project, "Justin Edit," for presenter Justin Brown.

Include the complete source footage package from /home/ga/Desktop/Video Assets/ and the soundtrack /home/ga/Downloads/ES_San Pedro - Sugoi.mp3 in the project so all supplied media remains organized and available for review and follow-on edits. Assemble the main cut in a sequence named "YouTube" matched to the primary presenter camera footage. Trim the unedited setup at the head and tail and cut out interior mistakes, false starts, or dead air so the spoken delivery flows smoothly without gaps.

Introduce the speaker near the opening with an on-screen lower-third graphic reading "JUSTIN BROWN," and layer the supplied ocean and waterfall footage as visual cutaways where they support the presenter's remarks without interrupting the spoken dialogue. Run the background music track across the full edited sequence, aligned cleanly to the end of the cut, while keeping spoken dialogue and music separated and balanced for clear speech. Follow /workspace/assets/justin_edit_visual_reference.md for the detailed visual and audio treatments across the edit.

Prepare the project for "YouTube 2160p 4K Ultra HD" export upon approval, and deliver the completed editable project at /home/ga/Documents/Justin Edit/Justin Edit.kdenlive (no rendered video file is required at this stage).
