Prepare Survey Data and Frequency Analyses

Prepare survey records and compare complete and missing-response frequencies.

Data analysis27 outcome criteria1 support file

Task instruction

You are a research analyst preparing a pilot survey dataset and a missing-response reporting check for a study on teacher emotional support and student emotional engagement. Using the questionnaire at /home/ga/Documents/Teacher Support Study/Document1.docx as your reference, set up a clean, populated survey dataset and produce comparative demographic frequency analyses under complete and missing-response conditions.

Structure the dataset in your statistical workspace around the individual closed-ended demographic questions and main-study statements in the questionnaire, clearing any stray placeholder entries that may be present. Each item needs a clearly identifiable variable, a descriptive label that accurately reflects the questionnaire item, and an appropriate measurement level distinguishing categorical demographics from ordered scale statements. Assign consistent whole-number codes with value labels for all printed response options, preserving the directional order of the agreement scale, and populate six sample respondent records with varied, valid coded responses across all questionnaire items.

Deliver two sets of frequency analyses covering all demographic variables from the questionnaire so the research team can compare how a missing entry affects reported counts and percentages. Each analysis should report valid and missing case summaries alongside labeled category frequency tables:

  • Complete Baseline: Frequency results across all six complete respondent records, showing six valid cases and zero missing cases for every demographic variable.
  • Missing-Response Comparison: Frequency results for the same six respondent records with a single respondent's gender response missing and all other demographic entries unchanged, showing five valid cases and one missing case for gender while every other demographic variable retains all six valid cases.

Leave the prepared dataset and both sets of frequency results open in your workspace for review, ensuring the reported distributions are consistent with the respondent records.
