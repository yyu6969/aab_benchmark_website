Reconcile a Business Bank Account

Reconcile a company ledger against a monthly bank statement.

Accounting8 outcome criteria1 support file

Task instruction

You are an accountant for HECTOR 2024 LLC. Reconcile the Chase Bank 8145 account in the HECTOR 2024 LLC company ledger for the period ending 01/31/2024 using the January 2024 bank statement at /home/ga/Documents/Reconciliation/Chase_8145_January_2024_statement.pdf.

Clear only the ledger transactions reflected on the statement, leaving any unmatched entries uncleared, so that the cleared balance matches the statement's ending balance with a $0.00 difference. Finalize the reconciliation and ensure the updated record is saved in the company ledger.
